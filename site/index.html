Gazi Mohd Shakil Imtiaz Fahad

I am a Tenured Lecturer in the Department of English at Mawlana Bhashani Science & Technology University, Bangladesh. I completed my Bachelor of Arts Honors in English with a CGPA of 3.81/4.00 and my Master of Arts in TESOL with a CGPA of 3.84/4.00 from Dhaka University, Bangladesh. My research examines digital literacy, technology-enhanced language teaching, and teacher self-efficacy in tertiary ELT contexts. I combine mixed-methods fieldwork with classroom interventions to produce evidence that directly informs pedagogy and policy. My work blends classroom-facing pedagogy with applied research to improve teacher self-efficacy and student outcomes. Feel free to reach out at fahad@mbstu.ac.bd.
